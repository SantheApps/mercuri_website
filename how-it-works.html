What it does

Answers and books

Every message lands in one inbox and gets a reply in seconds, day or night. When a customer wants a slot, it finds one and books it.

Reminds

Reminders go out at the times you choose, so fewer people forget.

Campaigns, sequences and one inbox

Your AI employee answers and books. These bring customers back: a class promo, a we-miss-you note, a welcome series, on the channel each customer actually reads.

Campaigns

  • SMS
  • WhatsApp
  • Email

Send a promo, a schedule change or a we-miss-you note to the contacts you choose, by SMS, WhatsApp or email.

Sequences

Set up a series once and it runs on its own: a welcome after a first visit, a check-in two days later, an offer after a week. Each step can go by SMS, WhatsApp or email, with waits in between.

One shared inbox

Every reply, whether to the AI, a campaign or a sequence, lands in one inbox you and your team share.

You’re in control

  • It answers from what you teach it.

    Your services, prices, policies and FAQs.

  • It sounds like you.

    Set its tone and how it introduces itself.

  • You see every reply.

    Every conversation is in your inbox.

  • Step in any time.

    Pause the AI for one conversation, or switch it off, and reply yourself.
