How your AI employee works

It answers your customers on WhatsApp, SMS and email, books them in and follows up. You decide what it knows, how it sounds, and when to step in.

Then it follows up

Before the class

A reminder goes out at the time you choose, so fewer people forget.

After the class

It asks for a Google review once the visit is done, and shows you which ones still need a reply.

Bring them back

Send a promo by SMS, WhatsApp or email, or set up a series once and it runs on its own.

You’re in control

  • It answers from what you teach it.

    Your services, prices, policies and FAQs.

  • It sounds like you.

    Set its tone and how it introduces itself.

  • One inbox for your team.

    Every reply to the AI, a campaign or a sequence lands in the inbox you and your team share.

  • Step in any time.

    Pause the AI for one conversation, or switch it off, and reply yourself.
