How your AI employee works

It answers your customers on WhatsApp, SMS and email, books them in and follows up. You decide what it knows, how it sounds, and when to step in.

A message comes in

Maya asks about tomorrow’s 7am class at 9:42 PM. Your AI employee replies in seconds, finds a spot and books her in, while you’re off for the night.

Before the class

A reminder goes out at the time you choose: a day before, two hours before, whatever suits your classes. Fewer people forget.

After the class

It asks for a Google review once the visit is done. Your Reviews page shows which ones still need a reply.

Bring them back

Send a workshop promo or a we-miss-you note by SMS, WhatsApp or email. Or set up a series once (welcome, check-in, offer) and it runs on its own, with waits between steps.
