What happens on every booking

Each message goes by WhatsApp, SMS or email. Bookings your team adds in the Wix dashboard get the same messages as online bookings.

  1. When they book

    A confirmation

    “You’re booked: Gel manicure with Lina, Thursday at 3:00pm.”

    The staff member they booked gets a heads-up too.

  2. Before the visit

    Reminders

    “Reminder: Gel manicure tomorrow at 3:00pm.”

    Minutes, hours or days before, at the times you set.

  3. If they cancel

    A cancellation message

    “Your Thursday 3:00pm is cancelled. Reply here to rebook.”

    Their reminders stop.

  4. If they don’t finish booking

    A nudge

    “Still want Thursday at 3:00pm? Finish booking here.”

    One message an hour later, with a link back to the booking.

Your messages, your rules

  • Written in your words.

    The client’s name, service, date and time, staff member and location fill in for you.

  • In your language.

    Write WhatsApp messages in English, Arabic or any language WhatsApp supports.

  • The right message for each client.

    A sequence checks the service, staff member, location or a label, then sends each client the message that fits. New clients can get your intake form; regulars don’t.

  • Replies land in one inbox.

    When a client replies, you and your team answer from the inbox you share.

Stays in sync with Wix

  • Contacts come in on their own

    New and updated Wix contacts appear in Mercuri, and we import the ones you already have.

  • Labels and consent stay matched

    Labels sync both ways, and marketing opt-outs go back to Wix.

  • Installed from the Wix App Market

    Pay through Wix, and run more than one Wix site from one Mercuri account.

  • Forms and orders too

    Wix Forms submissions and Wix Stores orders can send messages as well.

Questions owners ask

Do bookings my staff create in Wix get messages?add

Yes. Bookings your team adds in the Wix dashboard get the same confirmations and reminders as online bookings.

Which channels can I use?add

WhatsApp, SMS and email, chosen per message. In most countries you can keep your existing WhatsApp Business number and app (see which). SMS numbers are available in the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

When do reminders go out?add

At the times you set: minutes, hours or days before the booking. They can arrive up to 10 minutes either side.

What happens when a client cancels?add

They get your cancellation message and their reminders stop.

Can new clients get a different message from regulars?add

Yes. A sequence can check whether the client is new and send them your intake form link, while regulars get a plain confirmation.

Do I have to change how I use Wix?add

No. Mercuri works alongside Wix Bookings. Your services, staff and calendar stay in Wix.

What if a client books without a phone number?add

WhatsApp and SMS need a phone number, so make phone a required field on your Wix booking form.

What does it cost?add

Plans are $0, $29 or $99 a month. WhatsApp messages are $0.025 each from Mercuri, plus Meta’s own fee, which Meta bills you directly. See pricing
